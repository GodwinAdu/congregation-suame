# Suame - Product Overview

## Project Purpose
Suame is a comprehensive Next.js-based management platform designed for religious organizations, specifically tailored for Jehovah's Witnesses congregations. It provides integrated tools for member management, field service tracking, event coordination, and communication.

## Key Features & Capabilities

### Core Management
- **Member Management**: Publisher records, family relationships, emergency contacts, medical information
- **Role & Permission System**: Granular role-based access control with privilege management
- **User Authentication**: Secure login with JWT tokens and session management
- **Profile Management**: User profiles with service preferences and notification settings

### Field Service & Ministry
- **Field Service Tracking**: Daily field service reports, monthly summaries, and analytics
- **Territory Management**: Territory assignment and tracking
- **Public Witnessing**: Public talk scheduling and witnessing activities
- **Bible Study Tracking**: Bible study helper with manual reading plans and study tracking
- **School Management**: Student records and school-related activities

### Communication & Notifications
- **Push Notifications**: Web Push API with VAPID authentication, service worker integration
- **SMS Reminders**: SMS notification system with configurable reminders
- **Email Communication**: Email service integration for broadcasts
- **Broadcast System**: Multi-channel communication (push, SMS, email) with scheduling
- **Announcements**: In-app announcement system

### Administrative Features
- **Attendance Tracking**: Monthly attendance analytics and reporting
- **Event Management**: Event creation, scheduling, and coordination
- **Financial Management**: Expense tracking, transport fees, financial reporting
- **Duty Assignment**: Member duty scheduling and assignment
- **Shepherding**: Shepherding call tracking and management
- **Overseer Reports**: Overseer-specific reporting and analytics

### Data & Analytics
- **Activity Logging**: Comprehensive activity history and audit trails
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Field service analytics, attendance trends, engagement metrics
- **Vector Search**: AI-powered search using embeddings for intelligent data retrieval
- **Backup & Recovery**: Disaster recovery and data backup functionality
- **Export Capabilities**: PDF and Excel export for reports

### Technical Features
- **Internationalization**: Multi-language support (English, Traditional Chinese)
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-first UI with Tailwind CSS and Radix UI components
- **Dark Mode**: Theme switching support
- **Progressive Web App**: PWA capabilities with offline support
- **AI Assistant**: OpenAI integration for intelligent assistance
- **Location Services**: Interactive mapping with Leaflet for member location tracking

## Target Users
- Congregation members (publishers, pioneers, elders, overseers)
- Administrative staff managing congregation operations
- Shepherding committees
- Event coordinators
- Field service coordinators

## Use Cases
- Track personal field service hours and reports
- Manage congregation events and meetings
- Coordinate member duties and assignments
- Send targeted communications to congregation members
- Analyze congregation growth and engagement metrics
- Maintain member records and emergency contacts
- Schedule and track Bible studies
- Manage territories and witnessing activities
